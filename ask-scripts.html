Ask-scripts card

Curiosity first. Asking ≠ endorsing.

Adult

  • Have you tried ChatGPT (or another chatbot) for anything related to mood, stress, or mental health?
  • Was it more like looking up information, a pep talk, or a conversation?
  • Did it give advice you’d be uneasy having a friend take?
  • Reminder: a bot is not a crisis plan — if you’re in danger, call or text 988.

Teen

  • When you’re stuck late at night — do you reach for a person or an AI?
  • What feels good about it?
  • Is there anything you’d rather hide about how you use it?
  • Companion / romantic bots are not safe for kids — full stop.

Parent

  • Has your teen used ChatGPT, Character.AI, or a “my AI” feature to talk about feelings?
  • Want a short handout? AACAP Facts for Families #148 on AI chatbots.
  • “Show me how you use it” beats a phone raid.
  • Companions as a house rule: it is OK to prohibit them for minors.

Asking ≠ endorsing. Document AI exposure without pasting transcripts into the wrong systems.

PHI stays in the chart and the room — not in a consumer chatbot.
